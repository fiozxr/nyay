ABOUT / NYAY.AI

Justice begins
with a question.

Nyay is an AI-powered legal information and research companion designed around a simple idea: legal knowledge should be easier to understand before people have to act on it.

What we're building

Nyay helps people ask legal questions in ordinary language, organize the relevant issues, and discover useful legal context and sources. It is designed for citizens, students, researchers, and anyone trying to make sense of a legal problem.

What we're not

Nyay is not a law firm, does not represent users, and does not replace a qualified advocate. AI outputs can be incomplete or wrong. Important decisions should be checked against authoritative sources and, where appropriate, a qualified legal professional.

Why the name?

न्याय (Nyay) is commonly associated with justice. We use the word as a direction, not a promise: technology cannot decide what justice means for a person, but it can help make legal information less intimidating and more accessible.

Legal clarity, one question at a time.
